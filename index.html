Landlord Battle – Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026

Landlord Battle is a board game for Android published by Synanic Tactics (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. In short: games on one device or over Wi-Fi send nothing to us. If you choose to play online, our game server keeps your Play Games player ID, your gamer name, your rating and the results of your online games. We have no advertising and no analytics, and we never sell any data.

Information kept on your device

The name you enter for yourself, your game settings and whether you own the full version are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Wi-Fi games

When you host or join a game over Wi-Fi, your player name and your moves are sent directly to the other devices in that game on your local network, so the other players can see them. Nothing is sent over the internet or to us.

Online games

Online games (part of the full version) are played through a game server we run. They are optional: nothing below happens unless you create or join an online game, open the list of open games, or open the online leaderboard.

To play online you sign in with Google Play Games. With your permission, Play Games gives our server your Play Games player ID and your gamer name (the public name of your Play Games profile). We do not receive your email address, your real name or your contacts. Our server also checks with Google Play that you own the full version, using the purchase information Google Play gives the game.

Our server keeps:

  • your Play Games player ID and gamer name;
  • your rating, how many rated games you have played and the date of your last one;
  • a record of each online game played to the end: when it ended, its players (gamer names and player IDs), their final cash and their new ratings;
  • which Play Games account first used each full version purchase, so that one purchase can't be shared between accounts;
  • a technical log of sign-ins (player ID and gamer name) and errors, kept for at most 30 days.

During a game the server relays the moves between the players' devices. It does not keep the moves once the game is over. Your device's IP address is used only to keep the connection and to limit how many connections one address can open at once; we do not store it.

What other players see

Other players at your online table see your gamer name (shortened to seven letters) and your moves. The list of open games shows the gamer name and rating of the player who opened a game. The online leaderboard shows the gamer name, rating and number of rated games of the top players, once they have played five rated games.

Why we use it

We use this information only to run online games: to check that you own the full version, to seat you at a table under your gamer name, to keep ratings and the leaderboard, and to find and fix problems. We do not use it for advertising, we do not build profiles, and we do not share it with anyone, except as required by law.

Where it is stored, and for how long

The game server runs on Google Cloud in the United States, and the connection between the game and the server is encrypted. Your rating and game records are kept for as long as online play is offered, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. The sign-in log is deleted after at most 30 days.

Deleting your data, and your other rights

You can play on one device or over Wi-Fi without ever going online. To have your Landlord Battle online information deleted, or to ask what we hold about you, email synanic.com@gmail.com with your gamer name. We will delete your rating, your entries in the game records and the purchase link within 30 days, and the sign-in log deletes itself after at most 30 days; nothing is kept afterwards. If you play online again, you start as a new player. Depending on where you live (for example in the European Union) you also have the right to correct your information, to object to its use and to complain to your data protection authority.

Google services

Sign-in (Google Play Games) and purchases (Google Play) are provided by Google, which handles that information under the Google Privacy Policy. Payments are handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has played online, contact us and we will delete their information.

Changes

If the game starts handling information differently, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.
