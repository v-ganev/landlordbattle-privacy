Landlord Battle – Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026

Landlord Battle is a board game for Android published by Synanic Tactics (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. In short: we do not collect, store or sell any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not send anything to servers of ours – we have none.

Information kept on your device

The name you enter for yourself, your game settings and whether you own the full version are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Wi-Fi games

When you host or join a game over Wi-Fi, your player name and your moves are sent directly to the other devices in that game on your local network, so the other players can see them. Nothing is sent over the internet or to us. This happens only while you play a Wi-Fi game you chose to host or join. The other players' devices show your name for the duration of the game.

Purchases

The full version is sold through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. The game uses Google Play's billing service to check whether you have bought the full version, and Google processes that purchase information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If a future version works differently – for example, if online play with sign-in is added – we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.
